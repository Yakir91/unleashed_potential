## Brand Do's and Don'ts

### DO:
✅ Emphasize competition credentials
✅ Show training videos and photos
✅ Use professional, confident language
✅ Highlight structure and discipline
✅ Share competition experiences
✅ Display awards and placements
✅ Focus on results and reliability

### DON'T:
❌ Use cute/playful imagery excessively
❌ Overuse "fur baby" language
❌ Promise quick/easy results
❌ Downplay the work required
❌ Use overly casual tone
❌ Shy away from "protection work" terminology
❌ Apologize for classical methods
